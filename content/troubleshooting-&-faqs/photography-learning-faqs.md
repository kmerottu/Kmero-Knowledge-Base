# Photography Learning FAQs

## What should I learn first in photography?

A beginner should start with:

1. Camera controls
2. Exposure triangle
3. Focus
4. Composition
5. Lighting
6. Lenses
7. Editing
8. Photography practice

Learning the basics and practicing regularly can help develop photography skills.

---

## Should I use manual mode?

Manual mode gives the photographer complete control over aperture, shutter speed, and ISO.

However, beginners do not have to start with manual mode. Aperture Priority and Shutter Priority can help photographers learn individual settings while the camera manages other exposure adjustments.

---

## How do I get a blurry background?

To create more background blur:

- Use a wider aperture
- Use a longer focal length
- Move closer to the subject
- Increase the distance between the subject and background

A lens with a wide maximum aperture, such as f/1.8, can be useful for this effect.

---

## How can I improve my photography?

Practice photographing different subjects and experiment with:

- Different focal lengths
- Apertures
- Shutter speeds
- ISO settings
- Lighting directions
- Compositions
- Editing techniques

Reviewing your photographs and identifying what worked and what did not can also help improve your skills.
