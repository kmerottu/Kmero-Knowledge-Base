## What is lighting in photography?

Lighting refers to the amount, direction, quality, and color of light used to illuminate a photograph.

Lighting affects:

- Brightness
- Shadows
- Contrast
- Colors
- Texture
- Mood
- Subject separation

Good lighting can make a subject easier to see and can improve the overall appearance of a photograph.

---

## What is natural light?

Natural light comes primarily from the sun.

Examples include:

- Direct sunlight
- Overcast light
- Window light
- Golden hour
- Blue hour

Natural light is commonly used for portraits, landscapes, travel, street photography, and outdoor photography.

---

## What is artificial light?

Artificial light is created using equipment or electrical light sources.

Examples include:

- Flash
- Speedlights
- LED panels
- Studio lights
- Softboxes
- Umbrellas

Artificial lighting gives photographers more control over the direction and intensity of light.

---

## What is the difference between hard light and soft light?

**Hard light** creates strong shadows and higher contrast.

Examples:

- Direct sunlight
- Bare flash
- Small light sources

**Soft light** creates gradual shadows and smoother transitions between light and dark areas.

Examples:

- Overcast skies
- Large windows
- Softboxes
- Diffusers
- Umbrellas

---

## What is the best lighting for portraits?

Soft, directional light is commonly used for portraits because it can create natural-looking shadows and flattering highlights.

Good options include:

- Window light
- Softboxes
- Large umbrellas
- Open shade
- Golden-hour sunlight

A reflector can also be used to reduce shadows on the face.

---

## What is golden hour?

Golden hour is the period around sunrise or sunset when the sun is low in the sky.

Golden-hour lighting is often:

- Warm
- Soft
- Directional
- Low contrast

It is commonly used for portraits, landscapes, weddings, and outdoor photography.

---

## What is blue hour?

Blue hour occurs around twilight before sunrise or after sunset.

The light during blue hour is typically:

- Cool
- Blue
- Soft
- Low intensity

Blue hour is useful for:

- City photography
- Architecture
- Landscapes
- Night photography
- Long exposures

---

## What is front lighting?

Front lighting occurs when the main light source is in front of the subject and behind the photographer.

It can create:

- Even illumination
- Fewer shadows
- Clear subject details

Front lighting is useful for beginners because it is relatively easy to control.

---

## What is side lighting?

Side lighting comes from the side of the subject.

It can create:

- Stronger shadows
- More contrast
- Texture
- Depth
- Dimension

Side lighting is commonly used for portraits, landscapes, and artistic photography.

---

## What is backlighting?

Backlighting occurs when the main light source is behind the subject.

It can be used to create:

- Silhouettes
- Rim lighting
- Glowing edges
- Creative effects

When using backlighting, photographers may need to carefully adjust exposure so the subject does not become unintentionally too dark.
